## 1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data Idle collects, why, how it's used, who it's
shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Idle mobile app, web app, and
API.

## 2. Data we collect

| Category | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Name, email, phone, password (hashed), profile photo | Create and secure your account |
| Location data | Approximate location (for job search radius), precise location (only while sharing during an active job, opt-in) | Show nearby jobs; optional safety feature |
| Job data | Job posts, bids, checklists, messages, before/after photos | Core functionality of the marketplace |
| Verification data | Government ID image and selfie (sent to our verification provider), verification result | Optional per-job identity verification (Section 5) |
| Payment data | Payment method tokens (never raw card numbers — handled by our payment processor), transaction history | Fund and release escrow |
| Ratings data | Stars, likes, written reviews | Trust and safety |
| Device/usage data | IP address, device type, app version, crash logs, in-app events | Reliability, security, abuse prevention |

We do **not** collect more government-ID or payment data than our processors require,
and we do not sell your personal data.

## 3. How we use data

- To operate the marketplace: show jobs, process bids, run escrow, deliver messages.
- To verify identity when a job requires it.
- To keep the platform safe: fraud detection, abuse investigation, enforcing the
  Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
- To communicate with you: job updates, safety notices, receipts, support responses.
- To improve the product: aggregated, de-identified analytics.

We do not use your data to train third-party advertising profiles, and Idle does not
sell personal data to data brokers.

## 4. Legal bases (where applicable, e.g. under GDPR)

Contract necessity (running the marketplace you signed up for), legitimate interest
(safety, fraud prevention), consent (precise location sharing, ID verification,
marketing communications — each separately opted into), and legal obligation (tax and
law-enforcement requests).

## 5. Identity verification data specifically

5.1 When a job requires ID verification, your government ID and a live selfie are sent
directly to our third-party identity-verification provider over an encrypted
connection. Idle's own servers store only: whether you passed, when, and a reference
ID with the provider — **not** the document image itself.

5.2 The verification provider's retention period and security practices are disclosed
to you at the point of upload, and you separately consent to this transfer (see
`ID_VERIFICATION_CONSENT.md`). You can ask us at any time which provider we use and
how to request deletion from them directly.

5.3 The Poster of a job that required verification only ever sees a ✅ verified / ❌
not verified badge — never your document or the details on it.

## 6. Who we share data with

- **Other Users**, limited to what's needed for the job: your name, profile photo,
  ratings, messages you send, and (if you choose) a verified badge.
- **Service providers** who process data on our behalf under contract: cloud hosting,
  the payment processor (escrow), the identity-verification provider, push
  notification service, customer support tooling.
- **Law enforcement or regulators**, only when legally compelled (subpoena, court
  order, or to prevent imminent harm), and we will notify the affected User unless
  legally prohibited from doing so.
- We do **not** sell personal data, and do not share it with data brokers or
  advertisers.

## 7. Data retention

We keep account and job data for as long as your account is active, plus a limited
period after closure for legal, tax, and dispute-resolution purposes (typically up to
7 years for financial records, shorter for most other data). You can request earlier
deletion under Section 8, subject to any legal retention requirement.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: access the personal data we
hold about you, correct it, delete it, export it, restrict or object to certain
processing, and withdraw consent (e.g., stop sharing precise location) at any time.
Submit requests through in-app Settings → Privacy, or [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We will respond
within the time required by applicable law (e.g., 30 days under GDPR).

## 9. Children

Idle is not directed at, and may not be used by, anyone under 18. If we learn a child
has created an account, we will close it and delete associated data, subject to legal
retention requirements.

## 10. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest for sensitive fields, role-based access
control internally, and store payment data via a PCI-compliant processor rather than
on our own servers. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected Users and
regulators of a breach as required by applicable law.

## 11. International transfers

If you are outside the country where our servers are hosted, your data may be
transferred internationally. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g.,
Standard Contractual Clauses) for such transfers.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We'll notify you in-app of material changes before they take effect.
