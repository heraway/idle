## 1. Who we are and what Idle does

Idle ("**Idle**," "**we**," "**us**") operates a mobile and web platform that connects
people who want to hire help for short-term, low-barrier work ("**Posters**") with
people willing to bid on and perform that work ("**Workers**," together with Posters,
"**Users**"). Idle is a **marketplace and technology platform only**. Idle is not a
party to any agreement between a Poster and a Worker, is not an employer of any
Worker, and does not supervise, direct, or control how a job is performed.

## 3. The nature of the relationship between Users

3.1 **Independent contractors, not employees.** A Worker who performs a job for a
Poster is an independent contractor of the Poster, not an employee of Idle or (unless
the Poster separately agrees otherwise in writing) of the Poster. Idle does not
withhold taxes, provide benefits, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance for
any Worker.

3.2 **Idle does not vet the work itself.** Idle provides optional identity
verification (Section 8) and a rating/review system (Section 9), but does not perform
background checks, licensing checks, or skills assessments unless explicitly stated on
a specific job. Posters and Workers are solely responsible for deciding whether to
work with each other.

3.3 **No guarantee of outcome.** Idle does not guarantee that a job will be completed
to a Poster's satisfaction, that a Worker will be paid, that a bid will be accepted, or
that any User's conduct will meet any particular standard, beyond Idle's obligations
around escrow described in Section 7.

## 4. Posting and bidding on jobs

4.1 A Poster must accurately describe the job: task, location, expected duration,
hours per day they are willing to have someone work, number of workers needed, and any
requirements (e.g., ID verification, a specific license such as a driver's license for
jobs that require driving).

4.2 A Worker's bid is an offer to perform the job at the stated price. A Poster
accepting a bid forms a binding agreement **between the Poster and the Worker** — Idle
facilitates but is not a party to that agreement.

4.3 Jobs may not involve illegal activity, and may not require a Worker to do anything
that violates local law (including labor law, licensing law, or safety law).

4.4 Idle reserves the right to remove any job listing that violates these Terms, the
Community Guidelines, or applicable law, at any time, with or without notice.

## 6. Checklists, photos, and proof of completion

6.1 A Poster may define a checklist of tasks for a job. A Worker marks tasks complete
as they finish them and may optionally attach a photo to a completed task.

6.2 Workers may be asked to take a "before" photo of the job site before starting and
an "after" photo once finished. These photos exist to give both parties objective
evidence of the work performed and to help resolve disputes; they are visible to both
the Poster and Worker on that job and to Idle's admin/support team if a dispute is
raised.

6.3 Do not photograph any person without their knowledge and consent, and do not
include any child's face, any government ID, or any other sensitive personal
information in a job photo unless the job specifically and lawfully requires it.

## 7. Payment and escrow

7.1 When a Poster accepts a bid, the agreed amount is placed into escrow via our
payment processor before the job begins. Funds are **held by the payment processor**,
not by Idle directly.

7.2 Funds are released to the Worker when the Poster marks the job complete, or
automatically after a set confirmation window if the Poster does not respond and no
dispute has been raised (this window and its length will be shown in-app before a
Poster funds escrow).

7.3 Either party may raise a dispute before funds are released. A disputed job's
escrow is frozen pending review by Idle's support/admin team, who may release funds to
the Worker, refund the Poster, or split the funds, based on the evidence available
(checklist, photos, messages).

7.4 Idle is not a bank and does not custody funds itself; escrow is provided through a
third-party licensed payment processor. Processor fees, if any, will be disclosed
before you fund escrow.

7.5 Chargebacks/disputes filed directly with a card issuer instead of through Idle may
result in account suspension pending investigation, since they bypass the evidence-
based dispute process in 7.3.

## 8. Identity verification

8.1 A Poster may require that anyone bidding on a specific job first complete identity
verification. If required, a Worker must upload a government-issued ID and a live
selfie to a third-party verification provider before their bid is shown to that
Poster.

8.2 Idle stores only the **result** of a verification (verified / not verified /
expired) and a reference ID with the verification provider — Idle does not store the
raw image of your ID document. The verification provider's own retention and privacy
practices apply to the document itself, and are outlined at the point of upload (see
also `ID_VERIFICATION_CONSENT.md`).

8.3 A verification result may be shared with law enforcement if legally compelled
(e.g., subpoena, warrant) — see the Privacy Policy, Section 6.

## 9. Ratings, likes, and reviews

9.1 After a job is completed, both parties may rate each other (stars, an optional
"like," and an optional written review). Reviews must reflect genuine experience with
that job and may not contain harassment, discriminatory language, or personal
information about the other party beyond what is relevant to the job.

9.2 Idle may remove a review that violates this section or the Community Guidelines,
and may take action against accounts that manipulate ratings (fake accounts,
review-swapping, coercion).
