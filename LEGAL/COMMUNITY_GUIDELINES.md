# Idle — Community Guidelines

Idle works because people trust it enough to let a stranger into their yard, their
home, or their car. These guidelines exist to protect that trust. Violating them can
result in a warning, suspension, or permanent ban — see the enforcement ladder below.

## 1. Be honest

- Describe jobs accurately. Don't understate scope, hazards, or duration to get a
  lower bid, and don't misrepresent your skills or availability to win a job.
- Use a real photo and your real name. Fake profiles are removed on sight.
- Don't manipulate ratings — no review-swapping, no fake accounts, no pressuring the
  other party into a good review.

## 2. Be safe

- Meet in a public place first when practical, especially for a first job with a new
  User.
- Tell someone where you're going. Use the in-app location-share option during an
  active job.
- Never share your Idle password, and never be asked for it — Idle support will never
  ask for your password.
- If a job or a person makes you uncomfortable, you can cancel, block, and report at
  any time — you never have to explain yourself to the other User to do so.

## 3. Keep payment on-platform

- Fund and release payment through Idle's escrow. Don't ask or agree to be paid
  off-platform for a job posted on Idle — this removes the escrow protection for both
  sides and is against the Terms of Service.

## 4. No discrimination or harassment

- Don't decline, cancel, or review a job differently based on race, ethnicity,
  religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any other
  protected characteristic under applicable law.
- No harassment, hate speech, threats, sexual advances, or unwanted contact. This
  applies in chat, in reviews, and in person.

## 5. Protect minors

- Any job involving the care of a child (e.g. babysitting) must not be bid on by
  anyone under 18, and Posters are strongly encouraged to require ID verification for
  such jobs.
- Never photograph a child for a job checklist/proof photo without the parent/
  guardian's explicit permission, and never share such a photo outside the job thread.

## 6. Only real, lawful jobs

- No job that requires illegal activity, unlicensed practice of a regulated trade
  (e.g. electrical work requiring a license in your area), or that violates local
  labor law.
- No jobs that are actually recruitment for a pyramid scheme, unpaid "trial work," or
  anything that isn't a genuine paid task.

## 7. Respect the checklist & photo system

- Checklists should reflect the actual agreed scope — don't add tasks after work has
  started without discussing it with the other party in chat.
- Before/after photos should show the actual job site, unedited. Submitting a
  misleading photo is treated as fraud.

## 8. Enforcement

| Violation | Typical action |
|---|---|
| First minor issue (e.g. incomplete profile, late response) | In-app reminder |
| Policy violation (e.g. off-platform payment request, harassment) | Warning + content removal |
| Serious or repeated violation (e.g. fraudulent ID, discrimination, threats) | Suspension pending review |
| Severe violation (e.g. violence, sexual harassment, confirmed ID fraud, endangering a minor) | Permanent ban, and a report to law enforcement where appropriate |

Admins log every enforcement action with a reason (see `AdminAction` in the data
model) and Users may appeal through in-app support.

## 9. Reporting

Report a user or a job from their profile or the job screen. Include what happened and
when — reports are reviewed by Idle's Trust & Safety admin queue, generally within
24–48 hours, sooner for safety-critical reports.
