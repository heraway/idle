# Idle — Liability Waiver & Release

**You must read and accept this waiver before posting a job or placing a bid.**

> Template drafted for a portfolio/demo project; not legal advice. Enforceability of
> liability waivers varies significantly by jurisdiction (and some categories of
> liability — e.g. gross negligence, injury to minors in some regions — cannot be
> waived at all). Have this reviewed by a lawyer licensed where you operate before
> relying on it.

By accepting this waiver, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

## 1. Jobs happen off-platform, in the real world

Idle is a platform that connects Posters and Workers. The job itself — mowing a lawn,
braiding hair, watching a child, moving furniture, driving somewhere — happens in
person, at a real location, and is entirely between the Poster and the Worker. **Idle
is not present for, and does not supervise, any job.**

## 2. Assumption of risk

You understand that meeting and working with someone you found through an app carries
inherent risk, including but not limited to: property damage, personal injury,
disputes over payment or work quality, and, in rare cases, criminal conduct by another
User. **You voluntarily assume these risks** by choosing to post, bid on, or perform a
job through Idle.

## 3. Release

To the maximum extent permitted by law, you release Idle, its founders, employees, and
affiliates from any claim, demand, or liability arising out of or related to:

- Any injury, death, or property damage occurring during or as a result of a job;
- Any dispute between a Poster and a Worker, including over payment, quality of work,
  or job scope;
- The conduct (on or off the platform) of any other User, including any criminal act;
- Reliance on a rating, review, or ID-verification badge, which reflect the
  information available at the time and are not a guarantee of a User's future
  conduct.

This release does not apply to Idle's own gross negligence, willful misconduct, or
liability that cannot be waived under applicable law (e.g., certain consumer-
protection statutes).

## 4. Recommended precautions

Idle strongly recommends that Users:

- Meet in a public place first for jobs at a private residence, when practical;
- Tell a friend or family member the job location, expected duration, and the other
  party's Idle profile;
- Use the in-app "share my location" option during an active job;
- Trust their instincts — cancel and report if something feels wrong, before the job
  starts if possible;
- For jobs involving children (e.g. babysitting) or driving, require ID verification
  and check any credentials the Worker claims (e.g. a driver's license) independently
  — Idle's ID verification confirms **identity**, not a criminal background, driving
  record, or childcare qualification, unless a specific job explicitly states
  otherwise.

## 5. Insurance

Idle does not provide insurance covering injury, property damage, or loss arising from
a job. Posters and Workers are responsible for their own insurance (e.g., homeowner's,
renter's, auto, or general liability insurance) and should check whether it covers
gig-work scenarios.

## 6. Minors on a job site

If a job involves the care or presence of a minor (e.g., babysitting), the Poster is
solely responsible for determining the Worker's suitability, and Idle strongly
recommends requiring ID verification and an independent reference or background check
through a qualified third-party service before hiring for any childcare job. Idle's
identity verification is **not** a childcare background check.

## 7. Acknowledgment

By tapping "I Agree," you confirm you have read this waiver, understand it releases
legal rights, and accept it voluntarily.
