# Idle — Identity Verification Consent

**Shown immediately before you upload a government ID or take a verification selfie.
You must actively consent here; it is separate from the general Terms of Service.**

## Why we're asking

A Poster has marked this job as requiring **identity verification** for anyone who
bids on it, or you've chosen to verify your identity proactively to unlock jobs that
require it. This exists to give Posters a basic, real-world identity signal —
especially relevant for jobs at a private home, jobs involving children, or jobs
requiring a driver to identify themselves — so that in the event of theft, harm, or
another serious incident, there is a verified identity on file.

**Identity verification confirms who you say you are. It is not a criminal
background check, driving record check, or skills/licensing check**, unless a specific
job separately states otherwise.

## What happens when you consent

1. You will be asked to photograph a government-issued photo ID (e.g. passport,
   driver's license, national ID card).
2. You will be asked to take a live selfie, used to confirm the person holding the
   phone matches the ID photo (liveness + face-match check).
3. Both are sent directly, over an encrypted connection, to our third-party
   verification provider — **[VERIFICATION PROVIDER NAME, e.g. Stripe Identity /
   Persona]**. Idle's own servers never store the image of your document.
4. The provider returns a result: verified, not verified, or needs review. Idle stores
   only that result, the date, and a reference ID with the provider.
5. Only a ✅/❌ verified badge is ever shown to a Poster. Your document, your selfie,
   and the details on your ID (address, ID number, date of birth, etc.) are **never**
   shared with the Poster or any other User.
6. Idle staff do not have routine access to your document image. It may only be
   reviewed by authorized Trust & Safety staff in the event of a fraud investigation,
   or disclosed if legally compelled (see Privacy Policy, Section 6).

## Retention

The verification provider retains your document per their own retention policy
(disclosed at [PROVIDER PRIVACY POLICY LINK]), typically for a limited period required
for fraud-prevention and legal-compliance purposes, after which it is deleted. You can
ask Idle Support at any time for the provider's current retention period, or request
that we submit a deletion request to the provider on your behalf, subject to any legal
retention requirement they must independently comply with.

## Your choice

Verification is **entirely optional** unless you want to bid on a job that specifically
requires it. Declining means you simply won't see or be able to bid on jobs marked
"ID-verified bidders only." You may withdraw a previously-given verification badge at
any time in Settings, which will hide it from future bids (it does not retroactively
notify Posters on jobs already bid on).

## Consent

By tapping **"I consent — continue to verification,"** you confirm that:

- You are voluntarily providing your ID and selfie for the sole purpose described
  above;
- You understand it is sent to a third-party verification provider, not stored by
  Idle directly;
- You are the person shown in the ID and selfie, and the ID is genuine and unaltered;
- You understand submitting a fraudulent or altered ID is a violation of the Terms of
  Service and may be reported to law enforcement.
